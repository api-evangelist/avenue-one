# Avenue One

> Avenue One is a New York-based technology service platform and investment marketplace for
> institutional owners, buyers, sellers, lenders and borrowers of residential rental real estate.
> Founded in 2020, it connects institutional debt and equity capital to local operating partners
> and property owners across 21+ U.S. markets. Avenue One publishes **no public API, no developer
> portal, and no machine-readable contract** — its software runs as a login-gated product suite
> ("AvenueOS") for internal, builder, investor and partner users.

## APIs

No public API. Avenue One operates a private API host at `api.credit.avenueone.com` behind the
AvenueOS portals; it publishes no OpenAPI document, no interactive reference, and no developer
sign-up. Interactive docs paths (`/docs`, `/redoc`, `/openapi.json`, `/api/openapi.json`) all
return the same not-found response as a control path.

## Authentication

- [Auth0 OIDC discovery (identity tenant)](https://avenueone.us.auth0.com/.well-known/openid-configuration): OpenID Connect metadata for the tenant fronting the Partner Portal
- [Partner Portal sign-in](https://partners.avenueone.com/): 302 to Auth0 universal login; requires a provisioned partner account
